03 / UCL MSC RESEARCH

Efficient Long-Video QA.

Investigating how long-video question answering can use targeted multimodal evidence rather than processing every moment with the same expensive inference procedure.

← All workDiscuss research ↗

Concept illustration; not an extracted result or evidence figure from the dissertation.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Less processing. Better evidence.

01 / PROBLEM

Long-form video

Finding the short events relevant to a question within videos lasting tens of minutes or longer.

02 / METHOD

Evidence-oriented retrieval

Offline indexing, retrieval and selective inspection to reduce unnecessary visual processing.

03 / EVALUATION

Quality and resources

Comparing answer correctness, coverage and practical resource usage under controlled evaluation conditions.

PUBLIC RECORD

Research with qualifications.

The dissertation includes experiments, baselines and ablations. Numerical results are intentionally omitted from this first public draft while the final report and publication permissions are reviewed; this page does not claim a performance improvement or degree outcome.
