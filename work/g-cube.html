THE PROJECT

From business documents to technical requirements.

01 / CHALLENGE

Finding the dependencies

Identifying how questions, evidence and calculations depend on one another across business documents, then turning those relationships into implementable requirements.

02 / APPROACH

Designing the workflow

Mapping those dependencies to LangGraph nodes, shared state and execution order. Deep Agents support research tools, with explicit inputs, evidence reuse and review conditions.

03 / EVALUATION

Comparing complete workflows

Comparing the engineered Agent with the same workflow executed as a Skill, using a separate model to assess content coverage and analytical depth.
