02 / TEAM PROJECT · SEP 2026–PRESENT

Interspace 间隙.

A team-developed original-character AI companion exploring character consistency, memory and continuity across conversations. We jointly contribute to product design, engineering and testing, alongside the app's interface and visual identity.

THE EXPLORATION

What makes an AI character feel continuous?

01 / CHARACTER

Emotion and response

Designing and comparing flows for emotional appraisal, response strategy and reply generation, informed by character settings, previous interactions and current state.

02 / CONTINUITY

Memory and state

Integrated the emotion module with the web prototype backend, with isolated conversation state and memory, and consistent saving of replies and state.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Product and interface

Jointly developing app concepts, interface layouts and AI-assisted artwork, supported by a shared commercial strategy and engineering specifications.

CURRENT STATUS

Development and research.

We participate together across planning, design, implementation and testing. Current work iterates prompts, temperature and other generation parameters, and compares models to improve adherence to character settings and interaction requirements.

The integration has passed database and browser checks; character consistency and conversational quality are still being evaluated. Source code and internal commercial details remain private.
